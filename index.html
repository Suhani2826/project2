About Me

I am an undergraduate student working toward a Bachelor of Science degree at Tetr College of Business. My program mixes business studies with hands-on technology work, and it has taught me that the best way to learn something is to build it. This website is one of those builds: every page here was written by hand in plain HTML.

I am curious about how the products we use every day are made. When I open a website or an app, I want to know how it is put together, why it was designed that way, and how it could be better. That curiosity is what led me to take Internet Technologies and Web Design.

What I Am Learning

This semester I am learning how the web works from the ground up. So far I have practiced:

  • Structuring pages with the HTML5 skeleton and semantic elements
  • Marking up headings, paragraphs, lists, links, images and media
  • Building forms that send data to a server
  • Using Git, GitHub and GitHub Pages to publish my work
  • Checking my code with the W3C HTML validator

Outside the Classroom

When I am not studying, I like exploring new places, meeting people from different backgrounds and learning from the classmates I work with on group projects. I enjoy turning ideas into something real, whether that is a business plan, a presentation or a web page.
